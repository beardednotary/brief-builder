# BriefBuilder ASO research

Source: Applyra, App Store, US, en-US, 2026-11-16. Every number below came
from an Applyra tool call (`inspect_keyword`, `run_autocomplete`,
`check_metadata`, `simulate_metadata`). Difficulty and traffic are Applyra's
0-100 scores. The listing these findings produced is in
[listing.md](listing.md).

## What the research says

1. **The exact-intent terms are tiny.** "air force epb" has traffic 15;
   "epr bullets", "epb bullets", "air force eval" and "bullet writer" all
   return 8, which is Applyra's floor value (eleven keywords returned exactly
   8), so read it as "too small to measure", not as a real reading.
2. **The big terms mostly mean something else.** "epb" (92) is dominated by
   the EPB electric utility in Chattanooga, "epr" (96) by Brazilian toll and
   fireworks apps, "enlisted" (67) and "military" (72) by war games, "af" (60)
   by Anytime Fitness and Abercrombie, and "1206" (56) by Chinese rail apps.
   "epb" and "epr" are still worth having in the name and subtitle because
   difficulty is low and they are the words Airmen type.
3. **The real reachable volume is adjacent:** "usaf" (84, difficulty 24),
   "myeval" (59, difficulty 16) and "evals" (48, difficulty 12).
4. **Nobody owns "EPB" in the Air Force sense.** No app with EPB in that sense
   in its title appears in the top 20 for any term checked. The closest direct
   competitor is "EPR Writer", #1 for "epr bullets".
5. **New-app reality.** Applyra puts a new listing in tier 1 (Emerging) and
   flags "air" and "force" as out of reach on their own. They stay in the name
   for the phrases they build ("air force epb", "air force waps"), not to rank
   on "air force" alone.

word "briefbuilder" that nobody searches (kept as the brand), "air" and
"force" out of reach on their own, and "bullet", "writer", "sheet", "review"
and "log" held by bigger apps.
